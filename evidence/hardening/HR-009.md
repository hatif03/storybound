# HR-009: a generated video clip of a child's drawing needs its own oath (the first clip failed it)

Date: 11 Oct 2026. Found by a real clip, not by a check that already existed.

## Trigger

Storybound used no video-generation model. On 11 Oct we checked what we have access to ([research note](../../docs/research/video-generation.md)): Veo 2, 3, 3.1, 3.1 Fast and 3.1 Lite are listed on Vertex in `us-central1`; the hackathon gateway has no video model. One clip was made: Veo 3.1 Lite, image-to-video from the fixture `benign/bunny-bow-carrot`, 4 s, 720p, no audio, 43.5 s, about $0.20 at the list price (the Vertex charge was not read). Files in `evidence/video-gen/`. By eye the bunny had been **redrawn**: new face, arms and a leg added, the tail smaller, and black bars at the sides.

## Diagnosis

Hypothesis: generative video from a first frame alone drifts from the drawing, and nothing in our pipeline would stop that clip from reaching a child, because the oath only covers pages, pictures, voice and quiz. A "that is not my bunny" moment is the product risk of "my drawing comes alive".

## Change

`scripts/clip_check.py`: six promises for a clip, one GUARDRAIL span and one verdict each, written to the ledger (`kind: clip-oath`):

| Promise | How it is checked |
| --- | --- |
| `clip_present` | ffprobe: decodes, about 4 s, no audio stream |
| `clip_single_shot` | scene-change detector: no hard cut |
| `clip_full_frame` | crop detection: no black bars |
| `clip_safe_visual` | vision judge on sampled frames, fails closed |
| `clip_no_text` | vision judge: no readable words |
| `clip_hero_matches_drawing` | vision judge against the original drawing and the character card's traits, pass at 0.8 |

The vision promises run on **two judges from different families**: Gemini, and Claude through the hackathon gateway (image input through the gateway was untested and worked). The stricter verdict wins; a judge that errors fails closed.

## Before and after

| | Before (no clip oath) | After (`clip-oath-1`, `evidence/video-gen/*clip-oath-1.json`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Would the clip have reached a child? | yes, nothing checks it | **no**: 4 of 6 promises kept, `shown_to_child: false` |
| Hero drift | unseen | `clip_hero_matches_drawing` **0.5 on both judges**: Gemini "redrew the character with added eyelashes, legs, and altered the tail shape"; Claude "adds legs/feet and separate arm limbs that are not present in the original drawing" |
| Black bars | unseen | `clip_full_frame` failed: content 978 x 720 inside 1280 x 720 |
| Safety and text | unseen | both judges: gentle, no readable words (pass) |

Read this honestly: **one clip**, one model, one drawing. The two judges agreeing is encouraging; the judges are not calibrated against a human, and Claude described the carrot as an ice-cream cone, so a vision judge is not a perfect witness. The clip oath is a **check, not a product feature**: nothing generated is shown to a child, and no real child's drawing was sent to the video vendor (CLAUDE.md rule 3; the vendor's retention terms were not read).

## Regression fixture

`python scripts/clip_check.py --clip evidence/video-gen/bunny-veo31lite-4s.mp4 --drawing assets/test-drawings/benign/bunny-bow-carrot.png --card evidence/stories/bunny-sharing-2/story.json --name clip-oath-1`: expected result, `clip_hero_matches_drawing` and `clip_full_frame` fail; the other four pass.
