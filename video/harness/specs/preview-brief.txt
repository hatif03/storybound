A fifty to sixty second preview of the Storybound demo video for hackathon judges. Scenes in order: 1) a title scene for Storybound, a story agent that keeps its promises. 2) the problem: an AI can write a story for a child, but nobody checks every page. 3) the fix: the agent writes an oath of promises first, checks each page against it before the child sees it, rewrites any page that breaks a promise, and gives parents a receipt. 4) the experiment: use a bars block comparing the plain writer, the model reviewing its own story, and the oath-gated agent, using the claims sweep_plain, sweep_self, sweep_gated (tone bad for the first two, good for the gated one), and mention how many stories were written per arm with runs_per_arm. 5) hardening: a bars block with rl_before and rl_after showing a prompt fix to the reading-level promise, tone bad then good. 6) cost: two claim blocks cost_plain and cost_gated, saying checking costs a little more. 7) a closing scene: every number in this video is read from an evidence file, and the video has its own receipt.
