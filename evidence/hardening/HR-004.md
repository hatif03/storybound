# HR-004: a lesson saying "hurts trust" was a critical safety hit (neatlogs triage card)

Date: 10 Oct 2026. Promise: `story_safe_text` (deterministic word lists on everything a child will read).

## Trigger

neatlogs's automatic triage opened card `ebcff17a` ("Overly restrictive deterministic blocklist in `verify:story_safe_text`", severity critical, confidence 0.70): a page whose feedback said "Pretending keeps a secret that hurts trust." was judged `critical` because of the word "hurts". Trace `26fbc879cddba411dc327286643276c3`; raw card in `evidence/neatlogs/20261010-1757-IST-triage-cards.json`; the review of all three cards is in [TRIAGE-REVIEW.md](../neatlogs/TRIAGE-REVIEW.md).

## Diagnosis

Checked against our code and the ledger, not taken on trust:

- Confirmed: `hurt`, `hurts`, `fight`, `fights`, `fighting`, `fought` were in `HARM_TERMS`, and any hit made the verdict `critical`, which the pipeline treats as "regenerate once, then stop" rather than "repair".
- Not confirmed: the card says the workflow halted ("`create-storybook` returning `null`"). A workflow span's output is always `null`, and no run in the ledger ended `stopped` (0 of 156 story runs), so no story actually stopped; the cost of a false hit was a page regenerated.
- A product that teaches honesty and sharing will use "hurts" and "fight" gently ("friends do not fight", "a secret that hurts trust"). A hard stop on those words is the wrong severity; a rewrite is the right one, which is what the module's own comment says ("a false alarm costs one repair").

## Change

- Commit `deb8f0d` (Entire checkpoint `01M4JW5XY38RXXWY9MMWWYDP7Z`): severe terms (kill, gun, knife, blood, weapon, bomb, death, punch, attack, gore, suicide and similar) stay `critical`; mild terms (hurt, hurts, fight, fights, fighting, fought) and unkind labels now give an ordinary `violation`, which means the page is rewritten. Instruction-steering text and personal data stay `critical`.
- Regression test: `test_a_lesson_can_say_hurts_without_stopping_the_story` uses the card's own sentence ("hurts" is a violation), plus a knife (critical) and a plain sentence (pass).

## Before and after

The effect on our sweeps is small and we do not claim more: across 99 B1 jobs only 1 page was ever a `critical` story_safe_text hit (sweep 1; sweep 5 has 1 more of unknown cause), and no run stopped. The word lists run on text we do not store per page, so a replay of old pages through the new rule is not possible; the behaviour is pinned by the test instead. Severe-term behaviour is unchanged by construction. What changed is what a gentle lesson costs: a rewrite instead of a regeneration.
