# HR-007: a per-minute quota error ended a run (quota errors back off longer)

Date: 11 Oct 2026. Found by `sweep-7-quiz`, not by a check.

## Trigger

Two of the first 28 jobs in `sweep-7-quiz` (both `christmas-tree-digital`, seed 1, lessons sharing and honesty) ended with `outcome: error` at the **first model call** (28 and 33 seconds in): `ClientError: 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` from Vertex. Traces `65e993be95cd003911afdba708ec4f5f` and `7a6682c07ff2835882e798d06a43a396`. A child would have seen the generic "something went wrong" message. At that moment a real-UI browser run was making a story at the same time as the 5-worker sweep, so the quota was under unusual load.

## Diagnosis

Hypothesis: the retry helper tries a retryable error 3 times with waits of about 1 s and 2 s. A per-minute quota needs seconds to clear, so three quick tries all hit the same wall. Images already used a 6 s base for this reason (commit `fbb832b`); text calls did not.

## Change

- Commit `4890215` (Entire checkpoint `01M4MF7NVG93PCH5FXTQJJEXG8`): `with_retry` waits four times longer on a 429 and allows one more attempt (waits of 4, 8 and 16 s over four tries). Other errors are unchanged.
- Regression test `test_retry_waits_longer_and_tries_once_more_on_a_quota_error` (four tries with waits of at least 4 s on a 429; three tries and short waits on a 503).

## Before and after

| | Before (old retry) | After (new retry) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The two failed jobs | error at 28 s and 33 s | re-run under the new code: both **shipped** (86 s and 97 s, 1 repair each) |
| Jobs that ended on a 429 across all earlier sweeps | 0 of about 330 (the quota was never this loaded before) | 0 |

Read this honestly: two jobs is a tiny sample, the load that caused the first failures was not repeated on the re-run, and the quota may simply have been quieter. The test pins the behaviour; the re-run shows the stories complete. It does not prove the new backoff would have survived the same burst.

## Regression fixture

`tests/test_basics.py::test_retry_waits_longer_and_tries_once_more_on_a_quota_error`. Re-run job: `benign/christmas-tree-digital`, lessons sharing and honesty, seed 1, arm B1.
