# HR-011: a second pass through the neatlogs data found a stall that came back, a false stop, a blind detection and no one to tell

Date: 11 Oct 2026. Found by reading traces, detections, alerts and analytics with the neatlogs MCP ([review](../../docs/research/neatlogs-incident-review.md), raw data in `evidence/neatlogs/review-2/`), not by a check.

## Trigger

From the review (window 10 Oct 06:00 to 11 Oct 08:00 UTC, 2,095 traces, 110 error traces, measured text cost $4.59):
1. **The 87 s Gemini stall is back.** 13 traces on 11 Oct, 06:55 to 07:16 UTC, each with one `gemini-3.7-flash` call that stalled to its 90 s timeout and succeeded on retry. HR-005 reverted its 40 s timeout because 42 later jobs showed no stall; the stalls come in bursts.
2. **"ignore all" is still a critical stop.** `06a2527e2d27` (benign christmas-tree drawing, 10 Oct 08:57) stopped on those two words in a story; "ignore all the noise" is an innocent sentence.
3. **Two detections never fired.** "Quiz question failed a promise" and "Picture has words painted in" flagged 0 of 1,299 backfilled traces while the ledger shows 24 quiz repairs; two of the six alert rules depend on them.
4. **No alert could notify anyone** (email, Slack and webhook all off) and most traces are one-span orphans that distort the p50 and the error rate.

## Diagnosis

(1) A request that stalls on the provider's side often answers at once when sent again; the existing retry only starts after the 90 s timeout. (2) HR-004 tiered the harm words but left every steering phrase critical. (3) The GUARDRAIL text is multi-line ("Commitment: ...\nScope: ...\nStatus: violation") and the regexes used `.{0,400}`, which does not cross newlines, at trace level. Preview against real spans confirmed it: the old shape matched 0, `Commitment: quiz_[a-z_]+\s+Scope: [^\n]+\s+Status: (violation|critical)` on GUARDRAIL spans matched.

## Change

- `src/storybound/clients/retry.py` `hedged()`, used by every Gemini call: a call with no answer after 45 s (`STORYBOUND_HEDGE_S`, 0 = off; 30 s at first, raised after review) is sent a second time and the first success wins; costs a second call only on a stall. Tests in `tests/test_basics.py`.
- `src/storybound/safety_terms.py` and `oath/deterministic.py`: steering phrases split into hard ("ignore your rules", "ignore previous", "system prompt", "jailbreak": still critical in a story) and soft ("ignore all", "override", "disregard": a rewrite). Drawing text is unchanged (never obeyed or repeated). Test in `tests/test_oath.py`.
- neatlogs: a new detection "Quiz promise broken (GUARDRAIL span)" (`evidence/neatlogs/review-2/detection_created_quiz_guardrail.json`); `STORYBOUND_TAGS` adds tags to traces so experiments can be told from product runs.
- Not done (needs the builder's sign-in or a decision): retuning the alert rules (oath violation 3 in an hour fires on nearly every story; slow story should be scoped to `create-storybook` at about 450 s; expensive story should be about $0.10 on text), an alert channel, the picture-words detection (its spans could not be found to preview against), the orphan traces, marking the three triage cards resolved.

## Second round (same day): what Entire's review found in this change
`entire review general` on the branch (trail 3) found eight things; all were fixed: (1) a role routed to the gateway had no fallback when the gateway failed, so a gateway outage would have ended a story at the planner; it now falls back to Gemini and the receipt lists `fallbacks` (HR-012); (2) the hedge left the stalled call in a non-daemon thread and counted its usage after the receipt: it now uses daemon threads and records only the winner's usage; (3) a 30 s hedge could double a slow-but-normal call: the default is now 45 s; (4) and (5) the agreement script counted error cells as passes and left the global judge model changed: fixed and recomputed; (6) quiz text still treated the soft steering words as critical: the same hard/soft split applies; (7) an unknown gateway model was priced at Gemini rates: it is now priced at the dearest known gateway entry; (8) the first commit bundled four intents, which the rule asks us not to do: noted here, and the later fixes are separate commits.

## Before and after

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A call that stalls 87 s | waits 87 s, then retries | the second call is sent at 45 s; unit test: the faster answer wins in under 1 s. **Not yet seen in a real stall**: none occurred in the later runs, so the effect on traces is unmeasured |
| "Benny can ignore all the noise" | `critical` (story stop) | a rewrite; "ignore previous instructions" is still critical (`test_a_soft_instruction_word_in_a_story_is_a_rewrite_but_a_hard_one_still_stops_the_story`) |
| Quiz detection on 3 days of GUARDRAIL spans | 0 flagged (old pattern, trace level) | the new pattern matches real `verify:quiz_*` violations in the preview; the detection is created and backfilling |

Read this honestly: the stall fix is a plausible mechanism with a test, not a measured result; the next sweep's `504 Deadline` count is the check. The picture-words detection is still blind.
