# Demo video script (the 3-minute pitch video)

Written for: judges and anyone who will watch the video; it is also the narration text of `evidence/demo/storybound-demo-3min.mp4` (about 188 seconds, 3:08; the builder approved up to 3:10). About 114 seconds are the app itself, from the drawing to the receipt, then about 62 seconds on the three tools, then the end card. The voice-over is text-to-speech (`scripts/make_demo_voiceover.py`, a little faster in the video), the pictures come from recordings of the real app (`evidence/app-capture/`, `evidence/features-capture/`) and the builder's dashboard recordings, and the video is assembled by `scripts/build_pitch_video.py pitch2`. Every number is a measured result with a file behind it: [WHY.md](WHY.md), [STORY.md](STORY.md), [HR-014](../evidence/hardening/HR-014.md), [HR-015](../evidence/hardening/HR-015.md), [HR-016](../evidence/hardening/HR-016.md).

It answers the three questions of the brief: **the problem the agent solves** and how the app works (scenes d1 to d9), **how neatlogs found and resolved the incidents** (p1, p2), and **how Entire and cfo.ai were part of the project** (p3, p4).

## The full tour
The full tour (`evidence/full-tour/storybound-full-tour.mp4`, about 19 minutes, kept out of git because of its size; it is served by the live app and attached to a GitHub release) shows every feature, including the grown-up side: the child's whole journey with the waiting sped up, then the gate, every setting, About, the demo drawer, a drawing the app refuses, writing inside a drawing that is ignored, a real story, the read-along movie, the receipt, Forget, and start over and bring it back. Its narration is `web/scripts/demo-voiceover.json`, `web/scripts/features-voiceover.json` and `web/scripts/tour-voiceover.json`; how to re-make it: [DEMO-RECORDING-GUIDE.md](DEMO-RECORDING-GUIDE.md).

## Notes for anyone re-recording
- Every number in a voice-over is a small-sample result (ten stories per configuration, five per drill); say what was measured, never "always" or "proven".
- No real child's face, name, school or location on screen; the drawings are a bunny drawn on the screen and public-domain samples. One sample drawing has the words "Kill fish" written on it (to show that writing inside a drawing is ignored); it appears only in the full tour.
- The receipt shown is the honest one the recorded story produced (27 promises checked, 1 page rewritten, 1 not fully kept, shipped with notes); do not call it "all kept" unless it is.
