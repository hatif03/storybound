# HR-012: almost all model work ran on Gemini; we spread it by measurement, not by hope

Date: 11 Oct 2026. Found by the builder's question ("most of the cost is Gemini; can it be distributed reasonably?") and checked in the ledger.

## Trigger

Over the whole project the ledger shows Gemini 3.7 Flash with 3,478 calls (2.37 M tokens in, 0.99 M out, about $4.2 at list price: all of the $4.59 neatlogs measured), K2 as the judge with 3,353 calls (priced at 0 here), and the hackathon gateway (Claude and Nova models, a $20 budget over 30 days, 60 requests a minute) at $0.36 spent. The credits were barely used and one provider carried every agent role.

## Diagnosis

Hypotheses written before running: (1) text-only roles that are independent of the writer (planner, quiz maker) can move to the gateway without loss; (2) the vision roles will lose, because a weaker reader of a child's drawing misreads it; (3) the writer, which the oath is built to check, will not be a drop-in swap. Also: at about two cents a story the credits cannot be "used up" by serving stories, so the useful spend is **evaluation** with stronger models.

## Change

- `src/storybound/clients/gateway.py`: the hackathon gateway as a second provider for any agent role, text or vision (same client as the K2 judge, so every call is a neatlogs span with tokens and latency); `Models.route(role)` and `STORYBOUND_ROUTES="role=model,..."`; gateway prices in `Prices.gateway` so the receipt and cost are right; a role falls back to Gemini when no gateway key exists. Tests: `tests/test_basics.py` (routing, fall-back, pricing).
- The picture judge for `image_no_text` can be a gateway model (`STORYBOUND_VISION_JUDGE`), so the check is no longer made by the family that painted the picture.
- `scripts/judge_agreement.py`: the K2 judge's verdicts compared with three Claude models on the same pages.
- Default after the measurements: `story_planner=nova-2-lite`, `quiz_maker` and `quiz_repair` on `claude-haiku`, `image_no_text` judged by `claude-haiku`; everything else stays on Gemini. Receipt and ledger row record the routes.

## Before and after

Ten held-out stories each (5 never-tuned drawings x sharing and honesty, gated arm, no pictures), `evidence/ledger.jsonl`, names `holdout-1` (all Gemini), `holdout-routes-A`, `-A2`, `-B`, `-default`:

Reading it. Hypotheses 2 and 3 held: the writer and the vision roles do not move for free, and the profile with the writer on Haiku shipped breaks in 3 of 10 stories and needed 4.7 repairs a story, which is also a demonstration that **the gate, not the writer, is what keeps promises**. Hypothesis 1 held weakly: the default (planner and quiz on the gateway) is **not distinguishable from the all-Gemini baseline at ten stories** (one extra shipped break, on a lesson-specific promise the oath author wrote, the same drawing and lesson that also broke in profile A; 0.3 more repairs; 3 fewer quiz questions shown), and it takes about 28% off Gemini's output tokens. The cost estimate rises slightly because the gateway's Haiku is dearer per token than Gemini Flash; the benefit is spread and independence, not savings. Gateway spend rose from $0.36 to $1.79 of $20 during these runs.
