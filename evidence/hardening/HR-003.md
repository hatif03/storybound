# HR-003: the page writer kept overshooting the reading limit (prompt p2 to p3)

Date: 10 Oct 2026. Promise: `age_reading_level` (limit 14 words per sentence at age 6). Follows [HR-001](HR-001.md), whose "what is still wrong" section named this.

## Trigger

After HR-001 the page-by-page writer in B1 was still over the limit on 22 of 224 first-pass pages (9.8%) in `sweep-2-p2`, against 4 of 224 (1.8%) for the one-call baseline. In the trace of one failing page (`a32498d665231ab02f7218b8b54e9a3f`, scene 7) the pages sat at 12, 14, 13 and 14 words and the failing one had 15. A second, smaller trigger: one story shipped with a note because the check counted "peach-colored" as one 13-letter word.

## Diagnosis

Hypothesis, written before the change: stating the limit made it the writer's target, and a model aiming at 14 overshoots by a word about one time in ten. A target two words under the limit should leave room. For the compound word: the long-word check should split on hyphens.

## Change

- Commit `31022e7` (Sat 10 Oct, Entire checkpoint `01M4JVWCZYN3VJVDYM070ZX1A8`): the page writer is told `limit - 2` (`target_sentence_words`); the verifier's limit is unchanged; the one-call baseline prompt is unchanged, so B0 stays a fair control. Prompt version `p2` to `p3`. The long-word check splits hyphenated compounds.
- Regression tests: `test_writer_prompts_state_the_reading_limit_the_verifier_enforces` (page writer gets the target, baseline gets the limit) and `test_hyphenated_compound_is_not_one_long_word`.

Same 14 jobs (7 drawings x 2 lessons, seed 1, arm B1, 5 workers). Before: the `sweep-2-p2` rows for those jobs (code `ffc4208`). After: `sweep-4-p3` ([report](../reports/20261010-1752-IST-sweep-4-p3.md), code `31022e7`). Outputs are sampled, so the stories differ between runs.
