# HR-015: failure drills, and the four things they broke

Date: 12 Oct 2026. Our dashboard had few incidents, so we made some on purpose: `src/storybound/chaos.py` injects a failure (a stall, an error code, an outage) into one service for a run (`STORYBOUND_CHAOS=service:kind:value:probability`), the runs are tagged `chaos` in neatlogs, and each row in `evidence/ledger.jsonl` carries the setting. Five stories per drill (`scripts/run_experiments.py`), results in [20261012-comparison.md](../experiments/20261012-comparison.md).

## Trigger
The neatlogs dashboard held few incidents, and the agent had only met failures by chance. A promise to recover from failures needs failures to recover from.

## Diagnosis
Hypotheses written first: (1) a stall costs about the stall time unless the call is hedged; (2) a down judge stops every story safely but produces nothing; (3) an outage of a dependency is shown to the child as an error screen, not a refusal. The drills held (1) and (2) and refuted (3) for the safety screen.

## The drills and what they showed
| Drill | Failure injected | Result | What it found |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 / C2 | Gemini stalls for 100 s on 15% of calls, hedged retry on / off | 5 of 5 shipped both ways; mean 244 s against 294 s (longest 471 s against 415 s) | The hedge saves about 50 s on average; five stories each is too few to call it more |
| C3 | Gemini returns 503 on 40% of calls | 4 shipped, **1 shown a refusal** | An outage of the safety screen was shown to the child as "your picture was refused" |
| C3b | the same, after the fix | 4 shipped, **1 generic error** | A 503 that outlasted its retries in the character reader escaped as "something went wrong" |
| C4 | K2 (the judge) is down | **0 of 5 shipped:** 3 stopped, 1 refused, 1 error | One judge is a single point of failure; no story could be checked, so none was shown |
| C4b | K2 down, after the failover | 5 of 5 shipped, checked by the judge from another family ($0.129 a story, 5 times the usual) | The receipt says the judge changed |
| C5 | the model gateway is down | 5 of 5 shipped | The gateway roles (planner, quiz, picture-text check) fell back to another model; $0.016 a story, no harm seen |
| C6 | image model returns 429 on 80% of calls | 2 shipped, 1 shipped with notes, 12 pictures failed; the whole run took 23 minutes | The agent falls back to a second image model and ships text-only pages when pictures cannot be painted; slow when the image service is the one failing |
| C7 | K2 and the gateway judge are both down | 5 of 5 stopped at the try-again screen, nothing unchecked shown | The safe failure: no verdict, no story |

## Change and before and after: the four things found, and the fixes
1. **A page-JSON mismatch from a stronger writer** (X2, HR-014): Claude Sonnet's repair call returned the two answers as a `choices` list; the parser wanted `correct` and `wrong`, and 3 of 10 stories ended in an error. Found in neatlogs by opening the error spans; `writer.normalise()` now accepts both shapes and the error names the keys it saw. After the fix: 10 of 10 (X2b). Test: `tests/test_pipeline.py`.
2. **A judge outage was a dead end** (C4): the agent had one judge. Now a K2 error fails over to a judge from another family (`models.judge_fallback`), the receipt records the fallback, and a run where every judge fails stops at the try-again screen (C7).
3. **A service outage looked like a refusal** (C3): a screen that could not run was reported as unsafe. It is now `screen_unavailable`, and the child sees "Pip is having trouble right now. Please try again in a minute." (error code `service_unavailable`, documented in `docs/API.md`), never a refusal.
4. **A model error that outlived its retries escaped as a generic error** (C3b): `execute()` now maps any such error to the same try-again screen. Test: `test_a_model_service_that_fails_after_its_retries_shows_the_try_again_screen`.

## Limits, said plainly
Five stories per drill; the drills were run before and after the fixes on the same five drawings, so the "after" rows are a re-test of the same cases, not a fresh sample. The stall drill's saving (about 50 s) is within the spread of the runs. C6's 23 minutes is a real weakness: with the image service failing, the child waits for pictures that may not come; the text pages are ready much earlier, and a hard time budget for pictures is not built.

## Regression fixture
`python scripts/run_experiments.py --only C3b,C4b,C7`; unit tests for each fix in `tests/test_pipeline.py` and `tests/test_judge.py`.
