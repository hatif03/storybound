# HR-010: pictures arrived after the whole story, so a child read every page without them

Date: 11 Oct 2026. Found by looking at a live run, not by a check.

## Trigger

In the live browser run with the new `auto` picture policy (script `web/scripts/e2e-real.mjs --pictures auto`, 11 Oct) the script counted **0 of 8** pages with a picture at the moment each page was read, and in the first command-line run of the same policy (`evidence/runs/20261011-1110-IST-auto-pictures-bunny-sharing.json`, trace `e218e6be35a9077a9f0ca3199c5f9daa`) the **first picture arrived 278 s after the start**, 105 s after the last page, and the story finished at 381 s. Pictures were made in the media step, which ran after the whole story and the quiz. A child who started reading at about 85 s read all eight pages looking at "Pip is still painting this picture".

## Diagnosis

Hypothesis: nothing in the design needs the pictures to wait. A page's picture prompt exists the moment the page is accepted, and which pages to paint is known when the story is planned (the planner's importance scores). Only a page the whole-story gate rewrites afterwards needs its picture made again.

## Change

Commit "Pictures start while the child is still reading" (see git log): for the gated arm with media on, the plan (`paint.plan` event, and the `degraded` events for pages left unpainted) is announced right after planning, and each chosen page's picture starts the moment the page is accepted (two at a time, the existing per-minute image slots still apply). The media step then waits for those pictures, makes any picture whose page the story gate rewrote, and does the narration. Test: `tests/test_pipeline.py::test_the_agent_paints_the_chosen_pages_while_the_child_is_still_reading` (the plan comes before the first page; exactly the chosen pages are painted; the others are listed with the reason).

## Before and after

| Same drawing (`benign/bunny-bow-carrot`), lesson sharing, policy `auto`, gated arm | Before (`auto-pictures-1`) | After (`auto-pictures-2`, trace `fa20da316e4ce642035a12bbffb4f514`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pictures chosen | pages 1, 4, 6, 8 (importance 2, 3, 3, 2) | pages 1, 3, 5, 8 (importance 2, 3, 3, 2) |
| Plan announced at | 172.8 s (after the story and the quiz) | **25.1 s** (before page 1 was written) |
| First picture ready | 278.1 s | **70.6 s**, 20 s after page 1 was accepted at 50.9 s |
| Last picture ready | 380.9 s | 237.8 s (page 8 accepted at 220.4 s) |
| Story finished | 380.9 s | **277.3 s** |
| Promises | 26 checked, 4 pages rewritten, all kept | 27 checked, 2 pages rewritten, all kept |

Read this honestly: one run each, the planner chose different pages (its output is not deterministic), and times depend on the load on the picture service at that moment. The first-picture figure is the clear improvement; the shorter finish is partly the saved waiting and partly noise. The reader's page text for a page left unpainted on purpose now says "Pip saved the paint for the big moments" from the moment the page appears, because the reason is announced before the page is shown.
