# HR-016: pictures and judge calls were missing from the story's trace (the orphan traces)

Date: 12 Oct 2026. Found by the neatlogs "picture words" detection that never fired, by one-span traces on the dashboard, and by three neatlogs incident cards that read fragments as failures. First recorded as unresolved in [HR-011](HR-011.md).

## Trigger
In a diagnostic story with four painted pictures (trace `f238415f0de63afe58fcbf9a32ff4f2a`), two `illustrate` spans and every `verify:image_*` span were missing from the story's trace; the dashboard also showed hundreds of traces of a single span named `IFM/K2-Horizon-375B-A23B`, and the detection on picture-words verdicts had nothing to see.

## Diagnosis
Hypotheses written first: (1) the spans were never created; (2) they were created in worker threads that had no trace context; (3) they were created with a parent span that had already ended, and neatlogs exports a span whose parent has ended as a trace of its own. Hypothesis 1 was refuted (the child spans exist, as separate traces); a minimal thread-pool test showed hypothesis 2 was not the whole story (threads attached fine while the parent was alive); hypothesis 3 fit: a node span (`node.settle`, `node.verify`) ends when the node returns, but a judge call or a picture started inside it keeps running. The incident cards confirmed the effect from the other side: the "infinite repair loop" card linked three-span fragments (`illustrate` with its model calls and no story around them), not a loop.

## Change
`Run.task_context()` (`src/storybound/pipeline.py`) gives worker threads a copy of the context captured inside the workflow span, so work that outlives its node is parented to the workflow, not to the node. It is used for early pictures, the picture pool, and the oath engine's asynchronous judge calls (`task_context` in `src/storybound/oath/engine.py`). The story's span also records a request and a result now (lesson, age, arm, picture setting, repair cap; outcome, receipt line, counts), so a reviewer who opens it in neatlogs sees input and output instead of `{}` and `null`. Tests: `test_worker_threads_run_in_the_workflow_context_not_the_node_that_started_them`. Commits `74349c9` and `0bfeecc`.

## Limits, said plainly
- The audit runs (`--audit`) re-judge pages outside any story, so their windows hold hundreds of one-span and two-span traces by design; those windows cannot be used for this measure and were not.
- The "after" sample is three stories and one drill. It shows the fragments are gone in these runs, not for every code path; a new background thread would bring the problem back.
- The detection is correct on the span format but unproven on a real violation; a picture with painted words would have to occur (the vision judge flagged none in these runs).
- The hypothesis about the neatlogs exporter is inferred from behaviour, not from its source.
- The neatlogs trace export endpoint returned `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` when we tried to save the "before" trace, so the "before" counts are from the diagnostic session's notes and the dashboard, not from a saved file.
