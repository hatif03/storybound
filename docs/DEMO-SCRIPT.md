# Demo video script (the 3-minute pitch video)

Written for: judges and anyone who will watch the video; it is also the narration text of `evidence/demo/storybound-demo-3min.mp4`. The voice-over is text-to-speech (`scripts/make_demo_voiceover.py`), the pictures come from the recordings in `evidence/app-capture/` and the builder's dashboard recordings, and the video is assembled by `scripts/build_pitch_video.py pitch`. Every number is a measured result with a file behind it: [WHY.md](WHY.md), [STORY.md](STORY.md), [HR-014](../evidence/hardening/HR-014.md), [HR-015](../evidence/hardening/HR-015.md), [HR-016](../evidence/hardening/HR-016.md).

It answers the three questions of the brief: **the problem the agent solves** (scenes 1 to 5), **how cfo.ai and Entire were part of the project** (scenes 9 and 10), and **how neatlogs was used to find and resolve the incidents** (scenes 6 and 7).

## How the other video was made
The full tour (`evidence/full-tour/`) shows every feature, including the grown-up side: the gate, every setting, About, the demo drawer, a drawing the app refuses, writing inside a drawing that is ignored, start over and bring it back, a real story, the read-along movie, the receipt and Forget. Its narration is `web/scripts/features-voiceover.json` and `web/scripts/demo-voiceover.json`; how to re-make it: [DEMO-RECORDING-GUIDE.md](DEMO-RECORDING-GUIDE.md).

## Notes for anyone re-recording
- Every number in a voice-over is a small-sample result (ten stories per configuration, five per drill); say what was measured, never "always" or "proven".
- No real child's face, name, school or location on screen; the drawings are a bunny drawn on the screen and public-domain samples.
- The receipt shown in the app capture is the honest one the recorded story produced; do not call it "all kept" unless it is.
