# HR-002: gated stories took too long (pipelined review)

Date: 10 Oct 2026. Not a broken promise but a cost of verifying: the gate made B1 stories slower than the ungated arm.

## Trigger

In `sweep-1-p1` a gated story (B1) took 246.8 s on average against 180.5 s for the ungated baseline (B0), about 1.37 times as long, and a child would wait for the whole of it. Every page was written, then reviewed by the slow independent judge (K2), then repaired if needed, one page at a time.

- Trace (before the change): `264208295ada9b28a96d2e29b32fb41c` (bunny, sharing, B1, seed 1; 358.6 s). Export: `evidence/traces/20261010-1508-IST-sweep1-p1-bunny-sharing-B1-7-reading-level-repairs.json`.

## Diagnosis

Hypothesis, written before the change: the judge call dominates the loop and the writer sits idle while it waits. In the p1 loop the next page was not started until the previous page's review had returned.

## Change

- Commit `daec9ea` (Sat 10 Oct 13:40 IST): pipelined review. Pages are written ahead (at most `MAX_AHEAD = 3`) while the judge reviews earlier ones. A page is shown only after its review is back and clean, in order. If a review fails, the pages written after it are discarded and the page is repaired, so verification still gates what the child can see. The planner also runs beside the oath author, and the writer retries unparseable output. The receipt records `first_page_s`.
- Entire checkpoint: **none for this commit** (made in a session run from outside the repository, before Entire hooks were attached). We do not invent one.
- Ablation switch: commit `0ad1832`, Entire checkpoint `01M4JKPTNVGR2HQR4QK8PEM4GR`. `pipeline.py` now reads `STORYBOUND_MAX_AHEAD` (default 3, unchanged); `1` means sequential review, the old behaviour.

## Before and after

The sweep-1 to sweep-2 comparison is **confounded**: HR-001 changed the prompt in between and cut B1 repairs from 8.8 to 1.82 per story, and B0, which has no pipelining, also got faster (180.5 s to 150.3 s, probably provider speed). So the headline numbers below are context only, and the evidence for this change is the controlled ablation after them.

| Seconds per story (mean) | B0 | B1 | B1 / B0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sweep-1-p1` | 180.5 | 246.8 | 1.37 |
| `sweep-2-p2` | 150.3 | 142.7 | 0.95 |

### Controlled ablation: same code, same prompt (p2), only the lookahead differs

Same 14 jobs (7 drawings x 2 lessons, seed 1, arm B1, 5 workers each time). Pipelined = `sweep-2-p2` rows (`MAX_AHEAD=3`; the sweep process started at 14:18 from the code of `ffc4208`, and the git sha stored on each ledger row is just HEAD when the row was written); sequential = `sweep-3-ahead1` (`STORYBOUND_MAX_AHEAD=1`, started 15:07 from `7a13f29` plus the uncommitted switch). The models sample at temperature above 0, so the stories and the number of repairs differ between the two runs.

| | Pipelined (3 ahead) | Sequential (1) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seconds per story, mean (median) | 149.6 (122.0) | 230.6 (207.8) |
| Seconds to first page, mean (median) | 61.2 (61.7) | 53.3 (45.8) |
| Repairs per story (mean) | 2.36 | 2.79 |
| Tokens per story (mean) | 37,222 | 34,000 |
| Estimated USD per story (mean) | 0.0171 | 0.0153 |
| Stories shipped with a broken promise | 0 / 14 | 1 / 14 |

- Pipelining cut total time per story by about 35% on the mean and 41% on the median. It was faster on 11 of the 14 jobs and slower on 3 (`bunny honesty`, `christmas honesty`, `van sharing`). With 14 jobs and different repair counts per run this is a clear direction, not a precise number.
- It did **not** improve time to first page: the median was slightly worse (61.7 s against 45.8 s). Each page is still shown only after its review, and writing ahead competes with the judge for the same model quota. First page is therefore not a result of this change, and we should not claim it.
- Cost: about 2 cents more per 10 stories in estimated tokens (more calls written ahead and thrown away on rollback); estimates, not measured by neatlogs.
- Paired traces for one job (bunny, sharing, B1, seed 1): pipelined `a32498d665231ab02f7218b8b54e9a3f` (121.9 s, 1 repair), sequential `e0d0e072d74ef1db7186b0cb445d744f` (193.6 s, 0 repairs). Exports: `evidence/traces/20261010-1522-IST-sweep2-bunny-sharing-B1-pipelined.json` and `...-sweep3-ahead1-bunny-sharing-B1-sequential.json`.

## Seen along the way (not fixed here)

One sequential job (`van-crayon`, honesty) shipped with a note: `age_reading_level` on scene 5, "too many long words: peach-colored". The check counts a hyphenated compound as one 13-letter word, which is probably a false positive in the verifier, not a reading problem. It is one case; if it recurs it becomes HR-003 with a fixture.

## Regression fixture

- `tests/test_pipeline.py` (added in `daec9ea`): pages are accepted in order and exactly once, and a failed review discards the pages written after it and repairs it (tested with a fake judge).
- Job to re-run for timing: `benign/bunny-bow-carrot`, lesson `sharing`, seed 1, B1, with `STORYBOUND_MAX_AHEAD=1` and `3`.
